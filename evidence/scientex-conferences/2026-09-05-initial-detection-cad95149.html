About Conference

Prepare to be part of a dynamic global gathering — the Primary Healthcare & Medicare Summit 2026, set for November 09-10, 2026, in Bangkok, Thailand.

After the remarkable success of Primary Healthcare 2025, the 6th World Congress on Primary Healthcare and Medicare Summit is set for November 09-10, 2026 in Bangkok, Thailand. This premier event unites healthcare leaders, innovators, and experts to share breakthroughs, discuss emerging trends, and shape the future of primary healthcare together.

 

The conference motto, “Transforming Challenges into Opportunities for Better Health,” reflects the commitment of healthcare leaders to overcome barriers and improve health outcomes worldwide.

 

Engage in inspiring keynote speaker sessions, thought-provoking panel discussions, interactive workshops, and poster presentations showcasing groundbreaking research. The conference program provides an unparalleled platform for attendees to exchange ideas, build professional networks, and form partnerships that drive forward impactful change in primary healthcare systems.

 

Hybrid Format: For those unable to attend in person, our virtual platform ensures accessibility to sessions and engagement opportunities. This inclusive approach enhances our mission to advance primary healthcare worldwide.

 

By convening professionals from around the globe, a primary healthcare conference is more than just an event—it's a movement toward a healthier, more inclusive future. Together, we can transform the principles of primary care into action and ensure no one is left behind.

 

Call for Abstracts

 

We are excited to announce the Call for Abstracts for the 6th World Congress on Primary Healthcare and Medicare Summit, taking place in Bangkok, Thailand on November 09-10, 2026.

 

How to Submit: Submit your abstracts via the submission link or share your abstract directly through email at primaryhealthcare@scientexevents.com.

Review Process: All submitted abstracts will be assessed by the conference committee/program chairs and undergo a peer review process. You will be notified of the acceptance or rejection of your abstract within 3-4 business days after the review process.

 

Tracing Our Success: Primary Healthcare Conferences Through the Years

 

Primary Healthcare 2025 | Paris, France | November 17-18, 2025

The 5th World Congress on Primary Healthcare and Medicare Summit, held on November 17–18, 2025, in Paris, France, successfully brought together global experts, researchers, clinicians, and healthcare leaders under the theme “Revolutionizing Primary Healthcare: Multi-Specialty Approaches to Care.” We extend our heartfelt appreciation to all speakers, delegates, and contributors for their participation and support, which played a vital role in making this congress a meaningful and impactful event.

 

Primary Healthcare 2024 | Bangkok, Thailand | November 07–08, 2024

The 4th World Congress on Primary Healthcare and Medicare Summit, held on November 07–08, 2024, in Bangkok, Thailand, centered on the theme "Enhancing Healthcare Excellence Through Global Cooperation." The conference featured impactful discussions, cutting-edge research, and inspiring presentations from distinguished speakers, fostering collaboration, innovation, and progress in primary healthcare worldwide. Thank you to all speakers and attendees for making this event a resounding success!

 

Primary Healthcare 2023 | Dubai, UAE | November 13-14, 2023

The 3rd World Congress on Primary Healthcare and Medicare Summit, held on November 13–14, 2023, in Dubai, UAE, united global experts under the theme of advancing primary healthcare. The event featured insightful discussions, groundbreaking research, and collaborative initiatives, driving innovation and excellence in healthcare. Heartfelt thanks to all participants!

 

Primary Healthcare 2022 | Paris, France | November 07-09, 2022

The Primary Healthcare 2022 Conference, held in Paris, France, from November 07–09, 2022, centered on the theme "Promulgating the Outcomes and Research in the Field of Healthcare." The conference featured a vibrant gallery of presentations and insightful testimonials from participants, making it an inspiring and impactful gathering. We extend our gratitude to all participants for their valuable contributions, helping advance healthcare worldwide.

 

Primary Healthcare 2021 | Vienna, Austria | November 07-08, 2021

The Primary Healthcare 2021 Conference, held in Vienna, Austria, on November 07–08, 2021, focused on the theme "Healthcare: Our Vision and Strategic Priorities for the Future." The event brought together healthcare leaders, researchers, and practitioners to share insights, discuss innovations, and outline strategic priorities for advancing healthcare systems globally. We extend our heartfelt thanks to all attendees for their contributions to this enriching and collaborative experience.

 

Who can attend?

 

Healthcare Professionals: Doctors, nurses, and allied healthcare providers seeking to improve patient care and stay updated on best

practices in primary healthcare.

Researchers & Academics: Those involved in healthcare research and looking to present findings, exchange ideas, and collaborate on new projects.

Healthcare Administrators: Leaders and policymakers looking to advance healthcare systems and explore strategies for improving accessibility and efficiency.

Public Health Experts: Individuals working in public health who are interested in global healthcare challenges and solutions.

Medical Students & Graduates: Aspiring healthcare professionals eager to learn about the latest trends and innovations in the field.

Industry Leaders & Innovators: Those in the healthcare industry looking to showcase new technologies, treatments, and systems that can improve primary healthcare.

Health NGOs & Advocacy Groups: Organizations focused on improving healthcare access and quality globally, particularly in underserved communities.

Students and Trainees: Emerging professionals in medicine, nursing, and public health.

Industry Representatives: Pharmaceutical companies, healthcare equipment manufacturers, and service providers.

 

Mark your calendars and register now for an enriching experience that combines healthcare innovation with the charm of this iconic city, joining a vital movement to shape the future of primary care and global health systems!

Our conference aims to bring together researchers, clinicians, healthcare professionals, academicians, and industry experts from around the world to exchange knowledge, present recent research findings, and discuss emerging trends in medical and healthcare sciences.

The conference is open to researchers, doctors, professors, scientists, healthcare practitioners, students, industry representatives, policy makers, and anyone interested in advancements in medical and healthcare research.

Both physical and virtual conference formats are available and are scheduled on separate dates to provide greater flexibility for participants.
